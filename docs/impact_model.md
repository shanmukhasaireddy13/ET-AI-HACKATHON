# Meeting Mind Business Impact Model 📈

This model quantifies the ROI and operational efficiency gained by using Meeting Mind's 100% agentic AI workflows.

## 🧱 The Challenge
Engineering Leads and Product Managers spend significant "Shadow Time" on administrative tasks:
- **Task Extraction**: 15 min / meeting 
- **Tool Entry (Jira/Notion)**: 10 min / Jira ticket
- **Calendar Syncing**: 5 min / follow-up

For a typical lead attending **10 meetings / week**, this represents **~5 hours of lost engineering focus per week.**

---

## ⚡ The Solution: Quantified Benefits

### 1. Time Recapture (Post-Meeting Logistics)
- **Traditional Flow**: 30-45 minutes of manual task extraction, Jira entry, and Slack updates per meeting.
- **Meeting Mind Flow**: **5 seconds** (Agentic completion). 
- **Recapture**: ~5 hours / week per employee.
- **Yearly Value**: Assuming $120k salary ($60/hr), recapture is **$15,000 / year / employee.**

### 2. Reduced "Friction-to-Action"
- **Problem**: 20% of meeting items are "lost" or delayed because manual entry is deferred.
- **Impact**: Meeting Mind creates Jira/Notion items **immediately** while context is fresh.
- **Assumption**: 15% increase in team velocity due to reduced project lag.

### 3. Engineering Focus ("Flow State")
- **Benefit**: By offloading "Admin Task Context-Switching," engineers stay in deep-work states longer.
- **Value**: Research shows it takes 23 minutes to refocus after a distraction. Recovering 10 distractions/week saves **~4 hours of Flow State.**
